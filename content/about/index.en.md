Hello world! My name is Gabriel Moura, I am a geology student and besides being passionate about the earth, its processes and its evolution, I have always been very interested in programming and more recently an interest in statistics, data science, and so on. in 2017, I started the geology course at the Instituto Federal do Rio Grande do Norte (IFRN), and even before finishing the course, I enrolled in the geology course at the Universidade Federal do Rio Grande do Norte (UFRN).

At IFRN, I learned the basics of geology (Mineralogy, petrography, notions of geophysics, geochemistry, etc.) as well as tools that would assist me in the field, such as geoprocessing, which was the main focus of my final course project along with geochemistry.

At UFRN, I was able to deepen my geological knowledge and gain a totally different perspective on what the world is, the transformations that happen everywhere simultaneously. Along with this knowledge, I had the opportunity to participate in a junior enterprise where I performed geoprocessing and topography services and gained business experience in people management and in the role of president.
