Hello, reader! Welcome to my website, here I intend to write a little about geology and technology, as well as some other sub-areas that are of my interest. Here I intend to avoid topics that I don't have mastery of, and even if I don't, my basis will be referenced by people who have such expertise.

> You are creating a website/blog at a time when many people, professionals, and influencers are leaving this type of platform. — You must be asking

I understand this trend and know that when we analyze the data, the video platform, whether long or short, has a greater reach, is faster to consume, and is much more monetizable. At the moment, I prefer to improve my communication and writing skills while I do my studies and projects, perhaps adding (or even migrating) audiovisual content in the future.

I have look favorably upon video content, and some studies indicate that some people can absorb information better this way. I myself learned a lot about sedimentology, such as wave marks, sedimentary structures, silting of dams and rivers, precisely after watching videos of models on the internet, or even videos of floodgates being opened to remove sediments that are deposited over the years in a dam. These are very powerful tools for transmitting knowledge. This characteristic is precisely explored by social media algorithms in order to capture,

> One of the scarcest resources we have, which is **attention**. — Daniel Kahneman

Adding this to several other problems we have on social media, as discussed in [this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eu9uvBYsPXs) by Dr. K, I intend not to contribute to this psychological masturbation machine beyond what is necessary.

Furthermore, the creation of this website also came from the desire I had in my childhood to have my own site to talk about the things I consider most relevant. Over the years, I tried several times, and the project never advanced much, to the point where the first post didn't even go live, because many platforms did not support the content format I wanted to put, something that would only be possible if I created everything myself. That's when I challenged myself to create this site from scratch. Despite taking a few hours of my time, the site is exactly how I want it. It is being polished over time, as my study in webdev advances and the need to change something arises.

And that's what this is about, an amalgam of my knowledge and experiences. If you have reached this point and are interested in reading and learning a little about geology, data science, and related topics, you can find more about it here! See you in the next post.
